# Hephaestus / Scrollcraft build brief

Self-authored from the user's explicit delegation, not a claimed interview. The complete brief, eight creative decisions, act responsibilities, content, responsive behavior, motion timings, controls, backend contract and acceptance matrix are in [the execution blueprint](../../../docs/BLUEPRINT.md).

Grammar: exhibition-to-workbench. World: the volcanic atelier of Hephaestus. Central message: extraordinary things are made with intention. Peak: the visitor's selected object seals into a portable record. Six scenes lead from monumental encounter to possession. Desktop smoothing is optional; phone scrolling and reduced-motion access remain ordinary. Every visual transformation uses GSAP.

The final shape and exclusions are recorded in [the release audit](../../../docs/RELEASE-AUDIT.md); the post-ship fingerprint is in [the registry](../../FINGERPRINTS.md).
