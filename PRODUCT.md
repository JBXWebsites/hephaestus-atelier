# HEPHAESTUS / The Divine Atelier

## Register
brand

## Users
Design-conscious visitors and prospective JBX Web Studio clients discovering a portfolio experiment. They may arrive on a phone, know little Greek mythology, and have only a few minutes. The experience must demonstrate storytelling and working interaction without requiring an account, payment, or mythology knowledge.

## Product Purpose
A fictional divine atelier in which visitors encounter Hephaestus, inspect three imaginary relics, and forge a personal digital object. The central message is: extraordinary things are made with intention. The observable outcome is a visitor choosing a form, material and mark, creating a relic through a real server endpoint, reopening its share link, and downloading its archival print.

## Brand Personality
Sculptural, elemental, deliberate. A contemporary exhibition inside an ancient forge. Cinematic scale followed by human-scale control. Authoritative captions, brief lyrical headlines, concrete action labels.

## Anti-references
No SaaS feature-card template, pretend commerce, invented testimonials, fake scarcity, crypto/NFT framing, forced loading sequence, mandatory sound, game HUD, or collection of unrelated effects. No claims that original fictional relics are archaeological objects or canonical Greek myths.

## Design Principles
1. The visitor becomes the maker; every major section prepares or rewards that transition.
2. Material, typography, light and motion belong to the same imagined workshop.
3. Spectacle gives way to clear controls at the exact moment the visitor needs agency.
4. Choices produce visible and reproducible consequences.
5. Identify fiction and provenance honestly; real functionality is part of the craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals with keyboard controls, visible focus, semantic sections, readable contrast, 44px touch targets, 320px reflow, live status and equivalent reduced-motion access. Keep ordinary scrolling. Provide static artwork when WebGL cannot initialize. Do not claim certification or actual-device testing without evidence.
